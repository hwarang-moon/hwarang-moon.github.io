About

Hwarang Moon teaches liturgical theology and Christian education at Kosin University Graduate School of Theology (Cheonan, South Korea), where he also serves as Director of Planning. He holds a PhD in Liturgical Theology from Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary, and studied Christian education under Jack Seymour and worship under John Witvliet at Calvin Theological Seminary.

His research centers on formation through worship — the way liturgical practice shapes Christian identity and community. He has presented at the North American Academy of Liturgy (NAAL) for a decade and currently serves on its Nominations Committee.
