LITURGICAL STUDIES · CHRISTIAN EDUCATION Hwarang Moon 문화랑 Associate Professor of Liturgical Studies, Korea Theological Seminary (Kosin University) ABOUT Hwarang Moon teaches liturgical theology and Christian education at Korea Theological Seminary (Kosin University), Cheonan, South Korea. He holds a PhD in Liturgical Studies from Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary, where he studied under E. Byron Anderson, and studied worship under John Witvliet at Calvin Theological Seminary. He is the author of Worship in Conversation (Baylor University Press) and Engraved upon the Heart (Wipf & Stock), and his articles have appeared in leading international journals including Theology Today, Studia Liturgica, and Worship. He serves on the editorial board of Religions, an AHCI-indexed journal. He has presented at the North American Academy of Liturgy (NAAL) for over a decade. 문화랑 교수는 고신대학교 신학대학원(Korea Theological Seminary)에서 예배학과 기독교교육을 가르치고 있습니다. Garrett-Evangelical Theological Seminary에서 E. Byron Anderson 교수 문하에서 예전학(Liturgical Studies) 박사 학위를 받았으며, Calvin Theological Seminary에서 John Witvliet 교수 문하에서 예배학을 수학했습니다. Baylor University Press에서 출간한 『Worship in Conversation』과 Wipf & Stock에서 출간한 『Engraved upon the Heart』가 있으며, 국내에서는 『예배학 지도 그리기』, 『미래 교회교육 지도 그리기』, 『예배, 종교개혁가들에게 배우다』 등을 저술했습니다. Theology Today, Studia Liturgica, Worship 등 해외 유수한 학술지에 논문을 게재했으며, 학술지 『Religions』(AHCI 등재) 편집위원이고, 북미예배학회(NAAL)에서 10년 이상 발표해 왔습니다.
